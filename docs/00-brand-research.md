## The parent picture

Two sibling food brands, both rooted in **Eʋe (Ewe) / Volta-region Ghanaian cuisine**, both
operating in **Accra, Ghana**, owned by the same group. They are complementary, not
competing:

- **Giloz Restaurant** — the established, sit-down restaurant. Broader menu (Ewe + Ghanaian +
  Nigerian + continental), a sister beverage line, an evening/dining energy.
- **Sefofo** — the younger, nostalgic homestyle-meals brand. Emotive positioning ("we take you
  back home"), tighter comfort-food menu, memory-driven.

This platform unifies them under a light-touch **house brand** (working name **"Homefire"**)
while keeping each brand's identity distinct and switchable.

## Shared DNA (what unifies them)

1. **Eʋe / Volta heritage** — the cultural spine. Akple, banku, fufu, kontomire, abolo,
   one-man-thousand, light soup, groundnut soup, palm-nut soup, jollof, waakye, red-red.
2. **Accra, Ghana** — same city, two neighbourhoods (Pig-Farm & Dzorwulu). Real delivery zones.
3. **Instagram/TikTok-first** — visual, social, community-driven discovery.
4. **PDF menus today** — a clear upgrade opportunity: a living, searchable, filterable,
   photographed digital menu is a step-change for both.
5. **Ghana commerce reality** — WhatsApp is the default ordering channel; Mobile Money (MTN
   MoMo) and cards (via Paystack) are how people pay; prices are in **Ghana Cedis (₵)**.

## The opportunity (why this platform wins)

- Replace two static PDF menus with **one living, photographed, filterable menu** across brands.
- Give guests a **brand switcher** so the group markets itself as an ecosystem, not two silos.
- Add **cart → checkout → order tracking** on top of the WhatsApp habit (offer *both*).
- Surface **Naturia Beverages** as a first-class pairing/upsell.
- Lead with **award-level food photography and motion** — the brands are already visual;
  we give them a stage worthy of it.
